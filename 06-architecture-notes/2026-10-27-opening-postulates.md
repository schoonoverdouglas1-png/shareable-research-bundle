## P1 — Letter-codes mask diversity [H→D]
Meningococcal A, B, C, W, Y are shorthand for distinct capsular polysaccharides. [D] Capsule chemistry genuinely differs by serogroup and predicts vaccine approach (see P-seam below).

## P2 — "C" clustering as texture stereotyping [H→D, with a break in §1.1]
The essay groups Cystic Fibrosis, Cirrhosis, Cholera, Cryptococcosis as a "C" cluster grouped by visible texture rather than etiology.
- Tested three ways: (1) etymology dictionaries, (2) Greek roots by meaning, (3) transliteration logic.
- kystis = bladder/sac [D]; kirrhos = tawny, the colour of the diseased liver [D]; khole = gall/bile [D]; kryptos = hidden + kokkos = berry/seed [D].
- [LOGIC] These are four different roots with unrelated meanings. They share only the letter C in English transliteration — and in Greek not even that uniformly (kystis, kirrhos, kryptos begin with kappa; chole begins with chi). So §1.1's "shared roots" phrasing breaks as stated.
- [LOGIC] The break strengthens the thesis: the grouping is a spelling artifact, not an etymological family. That is exactly the stereotyping mechanism the essay names.

## P3 — Behavioral immune system and out-group blame [H→D, debated]
Disease cues trigger avoidance and out-group stereotyping, substituting heuristics for attack rates and transmission variables.
- [D] The behavioral-immune-system literature does report increased aversion/avoidance of unfamiliar and out-group targets under pathogen threat.
- [D, counter-evidence] Van Leeuwen & Petersen (2018) argue the system is designed to avoid infected individuals, not out-groups as such — so the out-group half of P3 is debated, not settled. Keep both readings; do not collapse them.

## P4 — Numerical reductionism in hSBA / R0 / MIC [H→D]
hSBA titers, R0, and MIC each compress a dynamic biological process into a single threshold or scalar. [D] hSBA ≥1:4 is used as a correlate of protection; R0 and MIC are by definition single-number summaries of heterogeneous processes. The essay's point is about compression, not that the numbers are useless — [GAP] how much compression is acceptable in each case is a separate judgment per index.

## P5 — The Pressman's Matrix as an integrative device [H]
His framing device: cross-reference linguistic classification, cognitive heuristic, quantitative index, and biological reality across domains (serology, pulmonary pathology, spread, defense, governance). Value to test in later turns: does each cell predict anything, or only describe? [GAP] not yet tested cell by cell.

## P6 — Maps, not territory [H→D]
Classification systems are necessary maps that oversimplify reality. [LOGIC] Follows from P1–P4 if those hold: every system above both enables action and hides heterogeneity. This is the essay's conclusion, not a separate empirical claim.

## The seam in the parallel [D + LOGIC]
Meningococcal lettering is NOT arbitrary the way C-clustering is:
- A: N-acetyl mannosamine phosphate; B: polysialic acid α2→8; C: sialic acid α2→9; W/Y: sialic acid with different linkages [D].
- MenB capsule resembles human neural cell adhesion molecule (NCAM) polysialic acid, is poorly immunogenic, and is unsuitable as a vaccine antigen — hence protein-based MenB vaccines (fHbp, NadA, NHBA, PorA) versus polysaccharide/conjugate MenACWY [D].
- [LOGIC] Hold both halves: some letter systems track biochemistry and predict coverage; others track spelling. The essay is strongest when it distinguishes those two kinds of letters rather than treating all lettering as equally arbitrary.

## My addition (Ember's, labeled)
A one-line test for any letter system in the Matrix: if changing the letter predicts a change in chemistry, vaccine, or coverage, the letter carries territory; if changing it predicts nothing, it is a spelling artifact. Apply that test row by row before the Matrix is treated as proven.
