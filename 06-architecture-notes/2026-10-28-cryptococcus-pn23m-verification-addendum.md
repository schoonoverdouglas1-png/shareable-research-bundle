## 1. Cryptococcus neoformans complex — VERIFIED [D]
- Serotype A = VNI, VNII, VNB — *C. neoformans* (var. *grubii*)
- Serotype D = VNIV — *C. deneoformans* (formerly var. *neoformans*)
- Serotype AD = VNIII — A × D hybrid
Source: MDPI/PMC, "Analyses of the Global Multilocus Genotypes of the Human Pathogenic Yeast Cryptococcus neoformans Species Complex" (2022): "C. neoformans (VNI, VNII and VNB, collectively called serotype A) and C. deneoformans (VNIV, commonly called serotype D), and their hybrids (VNIII, serotype AD)."
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9691084/

## 2. Cryptococcus gattii complex — CORRECTION to the email [D, supersedes the email's split]
The email claimed: B = VGI, VGII, VGIII; C = VGIV.
Independent sources say the split is different, and looser:
- Serotype B usually corresponds to VGI and VGII; serotype C usually to VGIII and VGIV.
- A systematic review adds that both serotypes B and C are associated with VGIII — so the letter-to-type correspondence is broad, not strict.
Sources: PMC, "Cryptococcus neoformans and Cryptococcus gattii, the Etiologic Agents of Cryptococcosis" ("VGI and VGII usually correspond with serotype B, and VGIII and VGIV with serotype C"); PMC systematic review of the *C. gattii* species complex ("Serotype B typically corresponds to VGI and VGII, whereas serotype C corresponds to VGIII and VGIV. However, both serotypes B and C are associated with VGIII").
URLs: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4066639/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11995670/

Consequence for draft P8 (the triple-C collision): the collision still holds, but its fungal leg must be restated — Cryptococcus serotype C is VGIII/VGIV (usually), not VGIV alone. The correction actually sharpens the essay's masking point: here a letter does not even map cleanly to molecular types, since VGIII sits under both B and C.

## 3. Pneumococcal serogroup 10 — VERIFIED, with a refinement [D]
- Serogroup 10 comprises four main serotypes, 10A, 10B, 10C, 10F, plus the recently identified 10D (the 100th serotype).
- 10A and 10C share a common backbone, as do 10B and 10F; side-chain substitutions create four structurally distinct capsular polysaccharides — which is why cross-reactivity between them is complex and asymmetric, as the email claimed.
- 10A is a vaccine target in both PCV20 and PPSV23 (confirmed in StatPearls/CDC vaccine lists).
Sources: Frontiers in Molecular Biosciences (2022), "Modeling of pneumococcal serogroup 10 capsular polysaccharide molecular conformations" (10A/10C and 10B/10F backbones; "complex asymmetric cross-reactivity"); NCBI StatPearls, Pneumococcal Vaccine (PCV20 and PPSV23 serotype lists both include 10A).
URLs: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/molecular-biosciences/articles/10.3389/fmolb.2022.961532/full and https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK507794/

Consequence for draft P9: the one-line test now has documented support on the pneumococcal leg — changing the trailing letter (10A → 10B) predicts a real change in capsule structure and in antibody cross-reactivity, but only partially, because the shared backbones keep cross-reactivity alive. "Partial," per Douglas's standing rule, not upgraded to full.
