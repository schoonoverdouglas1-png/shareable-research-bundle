CHAPTER 1
DOCTRINE, PURPOSE, AND SCOPE

1-1. Purpose. This manual establishes the doctrine of quantum medicine. It prescribes
how medical knowledge is to be gathered, tested, named, and applied so that the
practice of medicine becomes a better thing and never worse. The theme of this manual,
and of everything the author has thought on the subject, is stated once and governs
all that follows: CEASE LAWSUITS BY DOING THE RIGHT THING.

The author was taught medicine physically by two men, here named with intent: David
Cook and Salo Abid. Under Abid the author served as clinical assistant. Abid hired
him, showed him medical notes — including notes on extending life and on making
medicines work — and taught him the practice hands-on. What this manual prescribes
stands on what those two men taught.

1-2. Scope. This manual governs the author's thought process and no one else's. It
applies to four pillars and to nothing outside them:

    a. Tuberculosis.
    b. Pneumoniae (Streptococcus pneumoniae and its relations).
    c. Cephalitis. The standard term is encephalitis (inflammation of the brain).
       Where this manual writes "cephalitis," it means encephalitis unless a
       distinct definition is given on first use.
    d. Cephalopathy. The standard term is encephalopathy (dysfunction of the brain,
       including dysfunction arising from whole-body illness).

The working text of this manual is built clean. Historical atrocity material is
excluded from the working chapters by the author's directive; the dedication above
carries that weight instead.

1-3. The two-pass filter. All knowledge admitted to this manual passes two filters,
in order. Nothing is exempt.

    a. PASS 1 — SCIENTIFIC STANDARD. The Hippocratic filter is suspended. Each claim
       is tested on epistemics alone: falsifiable, controlled, reproducible,
       confounders accounted for, sample valid. What fails here is eliminated on
       science alone.
    b. PASS 2 — HIPPOCRATIC STANDARD. What survives Pass 1 is tested against consent,
       do-no-harm, and the patient before the doctrine. What fails here is buried.
    c. PUBLICATION. What survives both passes is shared, so that no loss that
       purchased knowledge was for nothing.

1-4. Operating formula. The author's thought process runs as follows, and the manual
runs it:

    a. Gather knowledge from all nations.
    b. Americanize it under Latin — the medical tradition — so it is never
       unethical through the Hippocratic method.
    c. Apply the scientific method and the Hippocratic method together.
    d. Integrate the result with the author's Unified Theory and Osmotic Theory.

The machine does not substitute for judgment. Every hard call remains the author's.

1-5. Method. The author names his method THEORETICAL BACKWARDS OSMOTIC FORWARD
THINKING: reasoning backward through the osmotic frame — pressures, balances,
impurities, and imbalances as the forces of the universe, of nature, and of
medicine — to move medicine forward toward a positive, bettering result.

1-6. Terms of this manual. Terms are defined before use. The manual recognizes four
terms for the human condition under care, drawn from different word-groups that
must be reconnected:

    a. Sickness — the condition as named and socially recognized.
    b. Illness — the condition as lived and experienced by the patient.
    c. Disease — the condition as clinically described.
    d. Cure — the resolution of the condition, defined separately within each
       word-group and reconciled by this manual.

   Rule of names. Common names answer to people; Latin names answer to description.
   Public disease names follow the current best practice against geographic and
   stigmatizing naming. Scientific Latin taxonomy — the Latinology, the namology —
   is not disturbed: descriptive epithets of place, color, and discoverer stand
   (e.g., fungi named by color). Where machines have assigned sequence-based names,
   those names are accepted as given.

1-7. Operational thesis. Lawsuits distort medicine: they bend naming, bend
attribution, and bend practice toward defense instead of care. Therefore: the more
lawsuits cease and the more people get help, the more the quality of care
automatically goes up. Every chapter of this manual will be traceable to that
sentence.

1-8. What this manual is not.

    a. This manual is not medical advice and does not direct the care of any
       patient. Treatment decisions belong to the patient and the prescriber.
    b. This manual does not validate its sources by assertion. Film-derived
       material is marked as film-derived. Accounts reported by the author are
       marked as the author's account. Hypotheses are labeled as hypotheses.
    c. This manual does not replace the author's judgment with the machine's.
       The machine runs the thought process; it does not own it.
